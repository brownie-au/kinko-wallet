2. Archive access for Ethereum, Polygon and BNB under verifiably free limits; archive block lookup/state evidence for PulseChain. Public headers alone do not close this gap.
3. Complete, bounded incremental discovery cursors going back before sold positions; indexer coverage/credits without paid dependencies. Current seeding may miss old or spam-misclassified tokens.
4. Historical HEX stake lifecycle, principal/yield/penalty methodology and other protocol valuations, without double-counting liquid balances.
5. Optional DEX history only after contract/network identity, quote currency, historical liquidity, volume, manipulation/outlier and timestamp checks. The tested DAI pool is evidence of candle access, not proof that DWB/DECI/PRVX/9INCH/9MM can all be valued.
6. Provider redistribution permissions appropriate to any broader public/commercial release. No new unrestricted licensing claim is made by a working API request.
